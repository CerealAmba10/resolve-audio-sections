Post Helper

Checking for Ducky’s Clip Organizer…

  1. Keep Ducky’s Clip Organizer open on this computer.
  2. On Fansly, RedGifs or Pornhub, start a post and click the site's upload button. Your clips appear: planned posts first, then your latest batch, then the whole library.
  3. Pick a clip. Planned posts also fill in their caption and list their post time and options on a card.
  4. Check everything, click the site's own Post button, then I posted it on the card. Ducky’s Clip Organizer logs it.

Hold Shift while clicking upload to use the normal file dialog instead.

While you browse your own Fansly profile or Statistics page, Post Helper reads the numbers on your posts (likes, tips and so on) and adds them to Ducky’s Clip Organizer. Nothing else is read, and nothing leaves this computer.
