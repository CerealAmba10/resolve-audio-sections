Post Helper

  1. In the Audio Sections planner, select posts and click Export batch.
  2. Click Import batch below, open the exported folder, select everything in it (Ctrl+A) and click Open. Import several site folders one after another if you like.
  3. Open the site and its new post form. A Post Helper card shows the next post: click Fill clip + caption, set the schedule and options it lists, check everything, and click the site's own Post button.
  4. Click I posted it on the card, and the next post appears.
