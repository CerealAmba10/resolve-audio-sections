Post Helper

Checking for Clip Organizer…

  1. Keep Clip Organizer open on this computer.
  2. On Fansly, RedGifs or Pornhub, start a post and click the site's upload button. Your clips appear: planned posts first, then your latest batch, then the whole library.
  3. Pick a clip. Planned posts also fill in their caption and list their post time and options on a card.
  4. Check everything, click the site's own Post button, then I posted it on the card. Clip Organizer logs it.

Hold Shift while clicking upload to use the normal file dialog instead.
