Upload Layout Recorder (Firefox)

This shows how each site's upload page works, so posting can be set up for you. It only records while you
have it switched on, and only on Fansly, RedGifs and Pornhub.

Install
  1. Unzip this folder.
  2. In Firefox, type  about:debugging#/runtime/this-firefox  in the address bar and press Enter.
  3. Click "Load Temporary Add-on..." and choose upload-layout-recorder.xpi. A page with these steps opens.

Record (once per site you use)
  1. Open the site, sign in as usual, and go to the page where you make a new post.
  2. Click the recorder button in the Firefox toolbar (top right), then "Start watching".
     It shows the page it is watching.
  3. Click EVERY button, tab, menu and option on the post page, one at a time, and let each menu open fully:
     media, caption, tags, price or "pay to view", who can see it, schedule, expiry, anything else there is.
  4. Add a short test video. Try adding several videos at once too, if the site allows it.
  5. Wait until the site says the video has finished processing. Post or schedule it only if you're happy to;
     you can delete it afterwards.
  6. Click the recorder button again, then "Stop watching". Move on to the next site.

Send it back
  Recorder button, "Review and save". Untick any screenshot you'd rather not share, click "Save zip", and send
  the zip from your Downloads folder. Save before closing Firefox: the recording (and the add-on) are removed
  when Firefox closes.

What it records, only while watching: clicks, the names of buttons, menus and fields, page addresses,
screenshots, and the field NAMES the site's pages send and receive (like "price" or "schedule time", never
what's in them). Never recorded: passwords, codes, anything you type, messages, payment details, or any
sign-in screen. Nothing is sent anywhere by the add-on.
