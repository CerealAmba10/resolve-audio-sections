Upload layout recorder

This shows how each site's upload page works, so posting can be automated for you. Do this once per site you use:

  1. Open the site below, sign in as usual, and go to the page where you make a new post.
  2. Click the recorder button in the Firefox toolbar, then Start watching. It shows the page it is watching.
  3. Click every button, tab, menu and option on the post page, one at a time, and let each menu open fully: media, caption, tags, price or "pay to view", who can see it, schedule, expiry, anything else there is.
  4. Add a short test video. Try adding several videos at once too, if the site allows it.
  5. Wait until the site says the video has finished processing. Post or schedule it only if you're happy to; you can delete it afterwards.
  6. Click the recorder button again, then Stop watching.

    When you've done every site: recorder button, Review and save. Untick any screenshot you'd rather not share, click Save zip, and send the zip from your Downloads folder. Save before closing Firefox; the recording is not kept after that.

    Recorded only while watching, and only on these sites: clicks, the names of buttons, menus and fields, page addresses, screenshots, and the field names the site's pages send and receive (like "price" or "schedule time", never what's in them). Never recorded: passwords, codes, anything you type, messages, payment details, or any sign-in screen. Nothing is sent anywhere.
