Upload layout recorder

For each site you use:

  1. Open it below and sign in as usual.
  2. Start a new video post with a short test clip.
  3. Open every option you normally use: caption, tags, price, audience, schedule and so on.
  4. Wait until the site says the video has finished processing. Post or schedule it only if you're happy to.

    Recorded on these sites only: clicks, names of buttons and fields, page addresses and screenshots.
    Never recorded: passwords, codes, anything you type, or any sign-in screen. Nothing is sent anywhere.

    When you've done every site, click the recorder button in the toolbar (or review here) to check the screenshots and save the zip. Save before closing Firefox; the recording is not kept after that.
