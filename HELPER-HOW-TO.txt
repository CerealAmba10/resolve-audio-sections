Ducky’s Clip Organizer + Post Helper

Ducky’s Clip Organizer (DuckysClipOrganizer.exe) keeps track of your clip folders, renames them, picks posting batches and
logs what was posted. Post Helper (a Firefox add-on) shows those clips when you upload on Fansly, RedGifs or
Pornhub. Everything stays on this computer.

Ducky’s Clip Organizer
  1. Run DuckysClipOrganizer.exe. It opens in your browser at http://127.0.0.1:47833 and keeps running quietly.
  2. Add each folder you want managed (nothing is tracked until you add it). Organize renames files in bulk:
       SFW                -> SFW 1, SFW 2... numbered oldest first
       SFW {N} {DATE}     -> keeps each file's own number and date: "take 7 2026-10-05" becomes "SFW 7 10-05-26"
       {N:3} pads the number (007). {DATE} is MM-DD-YY from the name, or the file's saved date if the name has none.
       {NAME} is the current name. The preview shows every change, and any number used twice, before you apply it.
     Posting history follows each clip through renames.
     Sort moves clips into a folder per year with a folder per month inside (2025 / 10 October), using the
     date in the name or the day the file was saved. Tick "Sort new clips automatically" to keep it that way:
     new clips are filed when the app starts, on Rescan, and every few minutes while it's open.
  3. Batch picks clips: never-posted first, then the longest-rested (with a little variety), never anything
     used recently. It copies them into a "Month Year" subfolder. Tick sites to plan their posts too.
  4. If it asks, let it download FFmpeg once: it makes thumbnails and checks each site's length limits.
  5. Works offline. It only goes online when you click "Check for updates" or "Download FFmpeg".
  6. Updates: click "Check for updates" at the bottom left. Updates come from the project's GitHub releases
     and are checksum-verified before installing.

Post Helper
  Install the signed post-helper.xpi you were given by opening it in Firefox and clicking Add.
  An unsigned copy says "could not be installed because it has not been verified" when opened. For a quick try,
  load it with about:debugging#/runtime/this-firefox, "Load Temporary Add-on...", choose the .xpi (removed when
  Firefox closes). For everyday use, ask for the signed copy.
  1. Keep Ducky’s Clip Organizer running.
  2. On Fansly, RedGifs or Pornhub, start a post and click the site's upload button. Your clips appear:
     planned posts first, then the latest batch, then the whole library.
  3. Pick a clip. A planned post also fills its caption, and a card lists its post time and options.
     Each batch has "Attach all" for upload boxes that take several files.
  4. Check everything, click the site's own Post button, then "I posted it" on the card. Ducky’s Clip Organizer
     logs it (if Ducky’s Clip Organizer is closed, the post is logged next time it opens).
  Hold Shift while clicking upload to get the normal file dialog instead.

Fill this page (RedGifs and Pornhub)
  First set each site's tags, categories, niches and so on in Ducky's Clip Organizer under Sites.
  RedGifs: after attaching the clip, click "Fill this page" on each step (audience, tags, niches, private), then
    click the site's Next yourself, and Publish on the last step. RedGifs has no scheduling.
  Pornhub: attach one clip or a whole batch, then click "Fill this page": every queued video gets its title, tags
    (at least 2) and categories (2 to 8, from Pornhub's list). Then set "When will this be published?" and
    visibility, tick the certification boxes yourself, and click Submit for Review.
  Post Helper never clicks Next, Publish or Submit, and never ticks Pornhub's certification boxes.
  Each new RedGifs and Pornhub post is linked to its clip, with its link, in the clip's details.

How your posts are doing (Fansly)
  Every time you open your own Fansly profile or Statistics page, Post Helper reads the numbers Fansly shows
  on your posts (likes, tips, replies...) and adds them to Ducky's Clip Organizer: see Activity, or a clip's
  details for its totals across every time it was posted. Posts made through Post Helper are linked to their
  clip automatically. It never reads messages, makes no extra requests and stores only the latest numbers plus
  one reading a day, all on this computer.

Fansly with FBuddy (PRO): open Bulk Posts, Add media, Upload new. Post Helper's picker opens: click "Attach all"
on a batch. Then click Copy next to captions on the Post Helper card, paste them into FBuddy's "Paste texts",
keep Sequential order, set the schedule and Post. Click "I posted all" so Ducky’s Clip Organizer logs them.
Fansly without FBuddy: post one at a time from the Planned row; the card shows each post's time and options.

Instagram, TikTok and Reddit are not supported on purpose: their terms forbid unapproved automation.
